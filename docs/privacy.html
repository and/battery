Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Battery Alert collects no data. Nothing leaves your phone. There are no servers, no accounts, and no internet connection required.

What the app does

Battery Alert monitors your device's battery level in the background and plays an alarm sound when the battery drops below a threshold you choose. The alarm stops when you plug in your charger.

Data collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. The app does not connect to the internet. No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking libraries are included.

Permissions used

The app requests the following Android permissions, all used solely on your device:

Battery state — to read your current battery level and charging status.
Foreground service — to keep monitoring active when the app is in the background.
Post notifications — to display a status bar notification while monitoring is running.
Receive boot completed — to restart monitoring automatically after your phone reboots.

None of this information is transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

The app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external services of any kind.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date. Given the nature of the app, we do not anticipate any changes.
